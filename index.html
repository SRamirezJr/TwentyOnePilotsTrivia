Twenty One Pilots Trivia

What album did Tyler Joseph write before he formed Twenty One Pilots?

No Phun Intended was the independent album writen by lead singer Tyler Joseph back in 2006! This predates their debut self titled album by 3 years!

Which Twenty One Pilots song has the longest music video?

The longest Twenty One Pilots music video and the longest music video in the world is the never-ending level of concern music video! The music video lasted 4,264 hours, 10 minutes, and 25 seconds.

What Twenty One pilots album has a bonus version with extra tracks?

Twenty One pilots only has one album that contains bonus tracks. The album is vessel which in certain regions has an extra version containing the songs: Glowing eyes, kitchen sink, forest, and lovely.
